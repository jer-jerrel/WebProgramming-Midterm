Tips for Your Visit

  1. Book a timed ticket online before you go.
  2. Come early in the morning or late in the afternoon for fewer crowds.
  3. Start with the Gallery of Honour and The Night Watch.
  4. Rent an audio guide or use the free museum app.
  5. Finish with a walk through the museum gardens.

Getting There

  • By tram or bus from the city centre.
  • On foot from the city centre, about 20 to 25 minutes.
  • By bike, which is the most Dutch way to travel.
