At a Glance

About the Museum

The Rijksmuseum is the national museum of the Netherlands. Its collection holds paintings, prints, sculptures and historical objects from the Middle Ages until today.

Opening Hours

Open every day from 09:00 to 17:00, including public holidays. Timed tickets are booked online. See the Plan Your Visit page.

Top Highlights

  • The Night Watch by Rembrandt
  • The Milkmaid by Vermeer
  • The Merry Drinker by Frans Hals

See all masterpieces

Location

Museumstraat 1, 1071 XX Amsterdam. The museum is on the Museumplein, right next to the Van Gogh Museum and the Concertgebouw.

A Short History

  • 1800 The national art collection opens in The Hague.
  • 1808 The collection moves to Amsterdam.
  • 1885 The current building, designed by Pierre Cuypers, opens.
  • 2013 The museum reopens after a ten year renovation.
